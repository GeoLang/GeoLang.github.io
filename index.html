GeoLang

Enterprise GIS. Natural Language.

A modular geospatial platform — 19 open-source Rust libraries for tile serving, routing, geocoding, data pipelines, and AI-powered spatial analysis.

Platform Components

Core Services

High-performance vector & raster tile server with PostGIS, MBTiles, PMTiles, and GeoPackage support.

ViewTopia

Ptolemy

Versioned geospatial feature store with branching, geoprocessing, and format import.

GeoLang AgentAI

Natural language interface to the entire platform. Powered by Letta persistence and Grok.
